# Audio recording

The alphabet voice is adapted from [Alphabet.oga](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Alphabet.oga) by Obsweant, licensed under [Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/).

The original 29-second recording was split into 26 letter segments, trimmed at the silences, normalized, and encoded as `alphabet-voice.mp3`. The adapted audio remains under CC BY-SA 3.0.
